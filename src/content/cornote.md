Reading a long PDF, report, or paper passively doesn't help it stick. Cornote was built to turn that passive reading into something closer to studying: it doesn't just hand you a summary, it makes you engage with the material.

The backend parses uploaded PDFs to extract clean text, then sends it to an LLM to generate a set of review questions based on the document's content. As users work through the document, they can add their own summaries at the bottom of each section, and Cornote also generates an overall AI summary of the full document for a quick recap.

The project pulled together document parsing, prompt design for question generation, and building a reading interface that keeps the user engaged section by section. It's an ongoing project, with more work planned on multi-document support and export formats.
