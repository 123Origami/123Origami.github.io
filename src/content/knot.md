Households buy a lot of things they only need once or twice: a drill, a projector, a folding table. Knot is a full-stack platform built to make it easy for a community to share what they already own instead of everyone buying their own copy.

Built with Django and a relational database, Knot handles user authentication, item listings, and a booking system so members can list items they're willing to lend, browse what's available nearby, and reserve items for a set window of time. Rental fees for paid listings are processed through a Payhero integration, and members can also suggest items they wish someone in the community had, turning unmet needs into future listings.

As the sole full-stack developer on the project, I worked across the stack: designing the data model for listings and bookings, building the Django views and templates, integrating Payhero for payments, and wiring up the booking flow end to end. Knot is still evolving, with more work planned on trust and rating features between members.
